**Shiori Route** means a route traced by bookmarks.

A bookmark marks a place where I once stopped: a question I was thinking about, a paper I was reading, a project I was working on, or an ordinary moment I did not want to forget. Over time, these scattered marks begin to form a path.

This website is where I keep that path.

It contains notes on my research, technical explorations, reading, everyday life, and the occasional thoughts that appear without a clear destination. Some entries may eventually grow into larger projects; others may remain as small fragments. Together, I hope they will become a record not only of what I have done, but also of how I have thought and changed.

I am currently a master’s student in computer science, with an undergraduate background in mathematics. My research interests lie at the intersection of secure computation, compilers, runtimes, networks, and distributed systems. In particular, I am interested in secure multiparty computation, batching, pipelining, and secure LLM inference.

Outside research, I spend much of my time thinking about questions that may or may not be useful, and following them through books, papers, essays, and whatever other materials I happen to find. I also enjoy Western calligraphy and video games.

This is not intended to be a polished account of a finished journey. It is a collection of bookmarks left along the way.
